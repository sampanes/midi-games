MIDI Output Lab

Find out what the computer can make the keyboard do: light pads, play its built-in synth, or switch sounds/presets. Only standard MIDI is sent: short quiet notes (velocity 32 or less, always released) and Program Change. Never SysEx, reset, or clock.

1. Connect

MIDI access has not been requested.

2. Before sending anything

  • Headphones/speaker in the AUDIO jack at very low volume (or unplugged).
  • ARP, Note Repeat, Scale, Chord, sequencer playback off.
  • Look at the pads and the screen while each test runs.
  • Try each output port in turn: [0], [1], [2], then Bluetooth.

3. Pad-light sweep

Sends one quiet note per pad, in pad order 1 to 16. Watch whether each pad lights up (and which colour). Velocity often selects the colour on pad controllers, so try a few velocities.

No sweep running.

4. Single test note

One quiet note. Useful for finding which port/channel plays the built-in synth (PATCH on).

5. Program Change

May switch the synth sound or the controller preset. This is the "put it in a known mode" test. Watch the screen. To undo by hand: hold PATCH and turn knobs 1-4 (sounds), or hold GLOBE and turn knob 1 (presets 1-8).
